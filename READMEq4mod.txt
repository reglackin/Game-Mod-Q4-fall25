This mod is a cooking game, but I was not able to complete all the planned deliverables.

Basic gameplay would have customers walk up to a counter and order a meal. The player would then gather the needed ingredients, and then make the meal using the monitors in the kitchen. The meal would then be delivered to the customer, who will eat and rate it based on how well made it was, how accurate it was to the order, and how long it took to make. Bad reviews drop the restaurant's star rating (starting at 5 stars) and if it drops to zero stars, the player loses. However, getting a good review can win back stars.

other things:
- in-game help screen pops up when tab is clicked
- ingredients float near the back of the (intended) kitchen, walk into them to pick them up.
